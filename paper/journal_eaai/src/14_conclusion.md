# Conclusions and future work {#sec:conclusion}

This paper presented a shared-state digital twin for orbital debris analysis in which a reliability-gated mixture of experts, calibrated by conformal prediction and isotonic regression, forecasts residual orbital lifetime and collision risk, and in which spacecraft health enters through operational status. It was evaluated on {{data.cohort.rows_out:,}} catalogued objects across two forward-in-time test periods, an official collision-avoidance benchmark and public spacecraft telemetry, with cluster-bootstrap intervals throughout.

The measured findings are:

1. In-context tabular foundation models are the most accurate lifetime arms in the later test period, by {{imp.re.o2016.tabicl_vs_xgboost_pct:.0f}} % against the best gradient-boosted baseline and {{imp.re.o2016.tabicl_vs_physics_pct:.0f}} % against the physics prior, and they give significantly the best collision-risk distribution, {{imp.cac.kumo_vs_persistence_pct:.0f}} % better than persistence. In the earlier period they are statistically tied with the physics prior.
2. The reliability gate did not beat the best single expert at either origin. It avoided the worst arm in both, and the fitting protocol mattered more than the gate's parameterisation.
3. Calibrated intervals cover {{re.o2016.rule.physics_only_conformal90.coverage_decayed:pct}} and {{re.o2011.rule.physics_only_conformal90.coverage_decayed:pct}} of decayed objects where the operational ±20 % window applied at the same horizon covers {{re.o2016.rule.physics_only_pm20.coverage_decayed:pct}} and {{re.o2011.rule.physics_only_pm20.coverage_decayed:pct}}.
4. Conformal coverage conditional on time degrades across a six-year forward test window, falling by {{re.o2016.tcov.physics_only.drop:.3f}} at origin 2016 while remaining stable at origin 2011.
5. Conditioning the area-to-mass ratio on an object's catalogue history reduces 72-day over-prediction from {{t1.by_horizon.72d.expected_over_observed_ci95.lifetime.ratio:.1f}}-fold to {{t1.by_horizon.72d.expected_over_observed_ci95.physics_history.ratio:.2f}}-fold.
6. Of the four couplings, breakup-family evidence helps the learned expert in one of two periods, cloud population is reproduced to about a tenth of a cloud without separating the experts, the status coupling has the expected direction but no significant gain, and the fourth cannot be tested on public data.
7. A catalogue covariate can leak the outcome through its availability. Adding it leaves point accuracy unchanged within noise and breaks interval coverage, so leakage of this kind is invisible to a mean-error check.

Future work follows directly from the negative results. The gate should be given descriptors of covariate shift and validated on a third period. The state-estimation step, which produces the largest effect in the paper, is currently Bayesian importance sampling over a physics propagator; replacing it with an amortised neural posterior trained on simulated propagations would make the system's strongest component a learned one, and we are pursuing that. On the collision-risk side, the gap between the best predictive distribution and the official point metric is a decision-theoretic problem rather than a modelling one, and a learned decision rule over the calibrated probability is the natural next step. Finally, the dated and hashed forecast released with this paper is the only fully blind test of the system, and scoring it against a later catalogue snapshot is the cleanest available validation.
